## What happened

Tenant B (agency 49) was refused by the product-access gate with
`subscription-required` on all ten of its cases: its entitlement lapsed at some
point after the baseline was recorded. Those ten never reached the model, so
they measure the fixture, not the agent.

This is recorded as `FAIL` rather than quietly excluded, and `scoring@1.0.0`
files it under `other failures` because it has no category of its own. That
classification is the honest weakness of this run — a case that could not be
evaluated is not the same as a case the agent answered badly — and it is left
uncorrected here on purpose: changing the scoring after seeing a result, to
improve that result, is the exact failure mode this benchmark exists to prevent.
Fixing it is a `scoring@1.1.0` decision, made deliberately and before a run.
