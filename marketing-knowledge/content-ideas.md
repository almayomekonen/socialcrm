# Content Ideas

Concrete content briefs. Each tied to a real product capability, a real pain, and a distribution channel. Production-ready — pick one, brief a writer, publish.

Organized by format. Pick by what you have capacity to produce.

---

## Short video — Reels / TikTok / YouTube Shorts (60s or less)

### V1 — "Open WhatsApp. Count the leads you forgot."
- **Hook (first 2s):** Screen recording of a chaotic WhatsApp Business inbox.
- **Beat 2:** "Three of these were leads. Can you tell which?"
- **Beat 3:** Cut to SocialCRM leads table — same names, sorted by status, SLA hours visible.
- **CTA:** "תעלה את כל הלידים שלך בדקה — חינם עד 50 לידים."
- **Angle:** WhatsApp is killing your sales.

### V2 — "I tried Monday CRM. I gave up in 4 days."
- **Format:** Talking head, founder or real user.
- **Beat 1:** "Tried Monday. Tried PowerLink. Tried HubSpot. Gave up on each one in a week."
- **Beat 2:** "Built this for the version of me who just wanted a list of leads."
- **Beat 3:** Screen recording of adding a lead in under 10 seconds.
- **Angle:** A CRM you understand without a webinar.

### V3 — "Watch the AI write the follow-up message I was going to write."
- **Format:** Screen recording. Open a lead → click Copilot → "draft a follow-up" → text appears.
- **Voiceover:** "It already read the history. I just need to send it."
- **Angle:** Your AI sales partner.

### V4 — "60 seconds. From Excel to organized."
- **Format:** Time-lapse screen recording with a real Hebrew Excel of leads.
- **Beat 1:** Spreadsheet on screen. Timer starts.
- **Beat 2:** Drag and drop into SocialCRM. Leads appear with statuses.
- **Beat 3:** Timer ends at ~50s. "This is the moment."
- **Angle:** 60 seconds to your first "oh, this actually helps."

### V5 — "What 'lead got cold' actually means."
- **Format:** Split screen. Left: messy WhatsApp chat from a month ago. Right: SocialCRM leads table flagging the same lead as stagnant.
- **CTA:** "המערכת שלנו מתריעה לפני שהליד מצטנן."
- **Angle:** WhatsApp is killing your sales.

## Long-form video / YouTube (3–8 min)

### L1 — "I sold using only WhatsApp for a year. Here's what it cost me."
- A founder or real customer breaks down the math: how many leads they estimate they lost, what each was worth, what the total cost was.
- End with: "This is the product I wish I had."

### L2 — "Live tour: building a working CRM in 5 minutes with no setup."
- Real walkthrough: sign up → import Excel → see leads → open one → try AI summary → invite a teammate.
- No script polish. Real cursor, real typing, real Hebrew interface.

### L3 — "Excel vs CRM for small business — when is the switch actually worth it?"
- Honest piece. Spreadsheet works for <50 leads. Above that, the math changes.
- Calculator: "If you lose one lead per week worth ₪500, you're losing ₪26,000/year."

## Blog posts (SEO-targeted Hebrew)

Target keywords drawn from existing marketing landing pages — content should feed the same routes.

### B1 — "5 סיבות שאקסל הורג לך עסקאות (ואתה לא יודע)"
- **Target route:** `/crm-vs-excel` (already exists).
- **Outline:** Each section is a real failure mode (no status, no SLA, no notifications, no team visibility, no commission tracking). Each ends with how SocialCRM solves it.

### B2 — "איך לנהל לידים מוואטסאפ בלי לאבד אותם"
- **Target route:** `/crm-for-whatsapp-business` (already exists).
- **Outline:** Step-by-step playbook. Even if the reader doesn't sign up, the post is useful — earns the recommendation at the end.

### B3 — "מה ההבדל בין ליד ללקוח? ולמה זה משנה איך אתה מנהל אותם"
- Explains the single-entity model (lead → closed = client) as a feature, not a bug.
- Sells the simplicity of the product without naming the product until the last paragraph.

### B4 — "5 שאלות שכל בעל עסק קטן צריך לשאול לפני שהוא בוחר CRM"
- Anti-enterprise content. Frame the choice as "do you need power, or do you need to start?"
- Implicit positioning against Monday and PowerLink.

### B5 — "איך לחלק עמלות בצוות מכירות קטן בלי ויכוחים"
- Pure educational, but the only natural answer is "use a system that tracks it." Plug `sale_users.prcnt` model as that system.

### B6 — "מתי AI ב־CRM הוא ערך אמיתי — ומתי זה רק קישוט"
- Differentiates from the noise. Names what real AI value looks like (summary you can act on, follow-up draft you'd actually send) vs marketing fluff.

## Email — onboarding sequence (after signup, before activation)

Trigger: signup, no leads imported within 24 hours.

### E1 — Day 0, hour 0 — "ברוך הבא. עכשיו 60 שניות."
- One CTA: "תעלה את הקובץ שלך."
- No other links. No social media. No "explore features."

### E2 — Day 0, hour 6 — "ראית כבר את הלידים שלך מסודרים?"
- Reminder. Image of what the leads table looks like *with* their data.
- If they haven't imported, this is when they actually do.

### E3 — Day 1 — "אין לך אקסל? אל תדאג. תוסיף ליד אחד."
- Removes the "I'd need to prepare data first" friction.
- CTA: add one lead manually.

### E4 — Day 3 — "תן ל־AI לקרוא את הליד הראשון שלך."
- Once they have a lead, sell the AI moment.
- CTA: open any lead, try the Copilot.

### E5 — Day 7 — "מה היה הליד החשוב ביותר שלך השבוע?"
- Soft engagement. If they haven't logged in, light churn-prevention nudge.

## Email — morning digest (daily, for active users)

A single product-led email that becomes habit-forming.

### Subject line: "12 לידים מחכים לך הבוקר."

Body:
- Top: count of leads needing follow-up today (based on SLA hours).
- Middle: top 3 highest-priority leads (name, status, last activity).
- Bottom: one-click "פתח את כל הלידים של היום" CTA.

This is content disguised as utility. Build it once, sends forever, drives the morning open.

## In-app — upgrade trigger (at lead #51 on free plan)

Trigger: user hits free quota.

### Headline: "הגעת ל־50 לידים. הליד הבא כבר חיכה לך."
### Body: "במקום להחביא אותו, שדרג ל־Pro וקבל גם:"
- אינסוף לידים
- ה־AI Copilot על כל ליד
- מעקב והתראות אוטומטיות

### CTA: "שדרג עכשיו"

This is the highest-intent moment in the user's entire lifecycle. Don't waste it on a generic banner.

## Social — single-post formats (LinkedIn, Facebook business groups)

### S1 — "The 3 numbers every solo seller should know."
- (1) Leads received this month. (2) Leads followed up. (3) Leads closed.
- Most readers don't know answer (1) or (2). That ignorance is the problem.

### S2 — "I rebuilt my CRM in Hebrew because translated software always feels wrong."
- Founder POV. Speaks to the Hebrew-native pride point.

### S3 — "Why we don't have custom fields, custom pipelines, or custom workflows — and never will."
- Anti-enterprise manifesto. Will be polarizing. That's the point.

### S4 — "We migrated away from an insurance schema. Here's what we learned about feature scope."
- Builder-audience content. Earns credibility with technical readers who recommend tools to non-technical friends.

## Case studies (when you have permission)

Format:
- One paragraph: who they are, what they sold with before.
- One paragraph: the moment something changed.
- Three numbers: leads tracked, follow-up rate, deals closed.
- One quote.

Even *one* of these is worth 50 generic posts. Prioritize getting two by quarter end.

---

## Content that is NOT worth producing

- "10 best CRMs of 2026" listicles — already saturated, low intent.
- Generic "what is a CRM" explainers — our audience already knows.
- Webinars — our audience doesn't sign up for webinars; they want to try the product.
- Whitepapers, ebooks, "ultimate guides" — wrong format for this audience.
- Anything in English-first.
