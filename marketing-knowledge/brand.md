# Brand

## What we are

SocialCRM is a Hebrew-first, AI-native CRM for small Israeli businesses whose leads arrive scattered across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook ads, spreadsheets, and notebooks.

We are not an enterprise CRM. We are not an insurance platform. We do not have multi-step pipelines, admin hierarchies, or a learning curve.

We are the tool that turns scattered leads into a clear list of people to call — so nothing falls through the cracks.

## Mission

Replace the feeling of switching between WhatsApp, Drive, spreadsheets, notes, and dashboards with one intelligent workflow that knows your leads and acts on them.

## Positioning

| When the user is choosing between…           | We win because…                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Excel / Google Sheets                        | Status, SLA hours, and AI follow-ups are built in — not a column you have to remember to update |
| WhatsApp + memory                            | Nothing gets buried in a chat; every lead has a home                                            |
| Enterprise CRMs (Monday, PowerLink, HubSpot) | Zero setup, Hebrew-native, no training required, designed for one-person sales teams            |
| Insurance-industry CRMs                      | We use sales language (`לידים`, `נציג`, `עסקאות`) — not `סוכן` and `תיק לקוח`                   |

## Core promise

A user who imports their Excel sees all their leads organized in one place — with status, name, and source visible at a glance — in under 60 seconds.

That moment is the product.

## Voice

- **Direct.** Short sentences. No marketing fluff.
- **Confident.** We tell the user what to do next, not what they could do.
- **Hebrew-native.** Not translated. Not localized. Written first in Hebrew, with the rhythm of how Israeli sales reps actually talk.
- **Anti-jargon.** No "synergize," no "pipeline optimization," no "customer success journey." Say "סגירת לידים" or "מעקב אחרי לידים."
- **Action over feature.** "תוסיף ליד תוך 10 שניות" beats "Lead management module."

## Voice — do / don't

| Do                                      | Don't                          |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| "תוסיף ליד"                             | "שמור"                         |
| "העלה לידים"                            | "המשך"                         |
| "כל הלידים במקום אחד"                   | "פלטפורמת ניהול לקוחות מתקדמת" |
| "נציג"                                  | "סוכן"                         |
| "ליד"                                   | "לקוח" (until the deal closes) |
| "עסקה" only when there's an actual sale | "הזדמנות" / "Opportunity"      |

## Visual identity

- **Direction:** RTL Hebrew throughout.
- **Typeface:** Assistant (Google Fonts). Hebrew-friendly.
- **Color rule:** Color carries meaning — status, priority, error, success. Never decoration.
- **No emojis in UI.** No playful illustrations. No decorative visuals that don't serve a function.
- **One primary action per screen.** Visual hierarchy is non-negotiable.

## What we explicitly are not

- Not an insurance platform (we scrubbed `cmsn`/`tfuca`/`סוכן` from the schema)
- Not an enterprise CRM with admin layers
- Not a multi-step workflow designer
- Not a marketing automation suite
- Not a customer support tool
- Not built for teams of 50+
- Not built for users who need training to start

## Brand personality (three adjectives)

**Clear. Fast. Trusted.**

If a design element, sentence, or feature does not serve one of those three — it does not ship.
