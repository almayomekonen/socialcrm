# Content Hooks

Short, scroll-stopping lines. Each is tied to a real product capability or real pain. Use in ads, email subject lines, landing-page headlines, social posts.

Hebrew first, English second only as reference. Never run the English in production.

---

## Hooks tied to the core pain

> כל הלידים שלך — במקום אחד.
> _All your leads — in one place._

> אף ליד לא ייפול יותר בין הכיסאות.
> _No more leads slipping through the cracks._

> ראית את הוואטסאפ? יש שם שלושה לידים שלא חזרת אליהם.
> _Checked WhatsApp? There are three leads you didn't get back to._

> שלוש פלטפורמות. שני יומנים. מחברת אחת. וליד שאבד.
> _Three platforms. Two calendars. One notebook. And a lost lead._

> לא איבדת את הליד. פשוט לא ידעת איפה הוא.
> _You didn't lose the lead. You just didn't know where it was._

## Hooks tied to the "Excel breakdown" moment

## How to use these

- **Ads (Meta, TikTok):** lead with the pain hook (Excel breakdown / cracks / "I never heard back").
- **Landing pages:** lead with the transformation hook (60 seconds / one place).
- **Cold email subject lines:** lead with the question hook ("Checked WhatsApp?" / "What is one lead worth?").
- **In-app upsell:** lead with the AI Copilot hook ("Let the AI draft it").
- **Retargeting:** lead with the Monday / Excel comparison hooks.

Never mix more than one hook per asset. One hook, one CTA, one screen.
