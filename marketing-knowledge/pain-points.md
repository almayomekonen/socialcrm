# Pain Points

The pain we sell against. Every word here is rooted in the actual users we built the product for.

---

## The structural pain

Leads arrive fast and from everywhere. There is no single place to see them all.

- A lead lands on WhatsApp, gets buried under 40 other messages, and is never followed up.
- Another lead comes in through an Instagram DM at 11pm and is forgotten by morning.
- A Facebook lead form drops a name into an inbox no one checks daily.
- A walk-in is written on a sticky note that gets lost by Thursday.

There is no way to know:

- Who has been called.
- Who is waiting for a response.
- What the status of any given lead actually is.
- What is working and what is silently dying.

The result: real money walks away, and no one knows exactly where.

---

## The emotional pain

This is what users actually feel. Use these words in copy.

- **Overwhelmed.** Too many leads, too many places, not enough time.
- **Out of control.** They know they are missing follow-ups. They do not know which ones.
- **Frustrated.** They built a system in Excel and WhatsApp that does not actually work.
- **Guilty.** They sense they are losing deals they should have closed.
- **Reactive.** Always catching up. Never ahead.

The user is not asking for features. They are asking for the feeling of control.

## What they currently do (and why it fails)

| What they do                                   | Why it breaks                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Copy-paste names into Excel                    | Forgets one. No status. No reminder.               |
| Send themselves WhatsApp messages as reminders | Gets buried in their own chat list.                |
| Sticky notes                                   | Lost by Thursday. Illegible by Friday.             |
| Phone notes                                    | Never opened again after the call.                 |
| Memory                                         | Fails the day there are more than three new leads. |
| Group WhatsApp with the team                   | Becomes noise. No one knows who owns what.         |

None of these scale past the first busy week.

## The specific moments where pain peaks

These are the scenes worth dramatizing in ads, emails, and landing pages.

1. **Monday morning.** 17 new leads from the weekend across three platforms. The user has 25 minutes before their first call. Where do they start?
2. **The "I never heard back" message.** A potential customer DMs a week later. The lead was real. It is now gone. The rep had it in WhatsApp and missed it.
3. **The Excel breakdown.** Three reps editing the same sheet. Conflicts. Overwrites. A row gets deleted. Nobody admits to it.
4. **The handover.** An agent leaves. Their leads are in their personal WhatsApp. There is no way to transfer them. The replacement starts from zero.
5. **End-of-month commission.** Two reps both claim they closed the same deal. Nothing in writing settles it.
6. **The cold lead.** A lead that was "interested" three weeks ago has gone silent. Nobody notices. Three months later the user discovers it in a buried chat.

## The pain we explicitly do not solve

Naming this is important — promising less is part of the brand.

- We are not solving complex multi-stage enterprise sales cycles.
- We are not solving customer support ticket management.
- We are not solving email marketing automation.
- We are not solving call-center routing.
- We are not solving accounting (we issue invoices; we are not an accountant).

If the pain requires a 12-month implementation, we are the wrong tool.
